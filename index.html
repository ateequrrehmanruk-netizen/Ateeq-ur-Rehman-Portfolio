03 — Selected Work

Things I
built

Academic projects where data, models and hardware had to actually work — from a 5,250-image food dataset to an Arduino device that steadies a shaking hand.

2024 — 2025·Final Year Project

Calorial — AI Nutrition for Pakistani Cuisine

An end-to-end system that recognises a traditional Pakistani dish from a photo and estimates its calories and macronutrients — built on FoodPK-50, a multi-view dataset of 5,250 images across 50 dishes.

  • Co-built FoodPK-50, capturing every dish from top, side and 45° angles with ingredients, gram weights, oil-level labels and nutrition from the Pakistan Food Composition Table.
  • Trained a multi-head ResNet-50 in PyTorch for dish, oil level, ingredients and nutrition; it reached 96.0% dish accuracy, 93.2% oil-level accuracy and 0.968 ingredient mAP.
  • Reduced per-gram calorie error by 41% (MAE 0.207 → 0.122, R² 0.92) using Focal Loss, label smoothing, cosine LR with warmup and EMA.
  • Shipped Calorial as a React Native app on a Flask API in Docker, with portion-size input and a human-in-the-loop correction flow.
PyTorchResNet-50Computer VisionFlaskDockerReact Native
